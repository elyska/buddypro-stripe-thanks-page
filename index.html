Don't have Telegram yet?

Telegram is a free messaging app. It takes about a minute to set up.

Step 1 — Install it on your phone

  1. Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Search for “Telegram”.
  3. Tap Install / Get.
  4. Open the app and sign up with your phone number.
  5. You'll get a verification code via SMS — enter it to finish setup.

Important: Set up Telegram on your phone first. You won't be able to log in on a computer without the mobile app.

Step 2 — Install it on your computer (optional)

  1. On your computer, go to telegram.org/apps.
  2. Download the version for your OS (Windows, Mac, or Linux), then install and launch it.
  3. Choose “Log in by QR code”.
  4. On your phone, go to Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device.
  5. Point your phone's camera at the QR code on your computer screen.

Tip: Once you're logged in on your PC, you can uninstall the mobile app if you wish — but we recommend keeping it for quick access on the go.

Once Telegram is installed, come back to this page and tap Open in Telegram above.
